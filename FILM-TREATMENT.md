# VOLD — BREACH

## Director’s treatment

A sealed world has been built to contain one can. The visitor follows a barely visible seam through aluminium, cold water and architecture. As the camera moves, the chamber becomes increasingly impossible. At maximum pressure, its walls separate along VOLD’s lightning-bolt geometry. Nothing explodes. The entire enclosure opens with surgical precision, exposing the product that has been controlling it all along.

The idea is **energy refusing containment**. The can supplies the world’s curves, its scale and its point of rupture. “NO RULES. JUST VOLD.” arrives only after the visitor has caused the breach.

One continuous, reversible 74-second editorial timeline. The timestamps below describe the intended viewing pace, not a timer that forces visitors onward. Normal scrolling, touch scrolling and the accessible scrubber control the same timeline. Stopping holds the shot; reversing scroll retraces camera, product and environment. Optional playback follows the same path.

## Reference study

The primary reference is [Verse’s 35-second Blender product commercial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IQQf7FEr668). I inspected its opening darkness, narrow travelling highlights, water framing the can, close surface views, silhouette/rim shot, energetic middle passage, wet pack shot and closing product repetition. Its useful lesson is the contrast between withheld information and tactile exposure: metal becomes legible through the shape of its reflections, and liquid supplies physical scale. VOLD will use its own compositions, a continuous camera path and a different central event. The reference’s ingredient orbits, splash arrangements, branding and closing lineup are not the storyboard.

[CIAO Energy](https://www.ciaoenergy.com/) informs continuous product-centred interaction. [Aether:1](https://aether1.ai/) informs entry into an authored world; its creators’ [production account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/08/06/building-aether-1-sound-without-boundaries/) supports separating camera paths, focal targets and scene choreography. These are production principles, not designs to reproduce.

## Shot 01 — A hairline in the dark

**Time / scroll:** 00:00–00:04 / 0–6%.

**Camera:** An almost imperceptible lateral dolly toward a red line. Tight lens; the line’s scale is unknowable.

**Environment:** A sealed, black chamber. Only one curved seam catches the light.

**Product state:** The can is present but unidentifiable; neither label nor complete silhouette is visible.

**Lighting:** A narrow red grazing source and a restrained silver glint. Most of the frame stays black.

**Materials:** Anodised black metal, polished aluminium at the seam.

**Typography:** Only a small “Scroll to enter” instruction and optional sound control.

**Sound:** Silence by default. If enabled, a low, distant mechanical pressure tone.

**Scroll interaction:** Every small scroll reveals a little more curvature. No timed entrance animation to wait through.

**Transition:** The glint lengthens into a rim; the lens tracks along it rather than cutting away.

## Shot 02 — The crown

**Time / scroll:** 00:04–00:10 / 6–14%.

**Camera:** A shallow crane over the upper rim, changing from grazing incidence to an oblique macro view.

**Environment:** The surrounding chamber remains out of focus and largely invisible.

**Product state:** Rolled lip, recessed lid and pull tab appear as pieces of a larger machine.

**Lighting:** Long white strip reflections travel across the aluminium; a fine red edge remains behind them.

**Materials:** Brushed lid, polished lip, stamped pull tab with real thickness and a visible opening.

**Typography:** None.

**Sound:** A delicate metallic resonance, proportional to camera travel.

**Scroll interaction:** The visitor controls the speed of the travelling reflection and the crane move together.

**Transition:** The lens descends over the shoulder toward a single bead of water.

## Shot 03 — Cold enough to touch

**Time / scroll:** 00:10–00:17 / 14–23%.

**Camera:** Extreme close tracking down the curved label. A deliberate focal target shift from rim to condensation.

**Environment:** Distant structural reflections appear inside the droplet highlights.

**Product state:** Ink grain, coating, droplets and a fragment of the VOLD mark; never the whole pack.

**Lighting:** Cool-white key with controlled red reflection, shaped to distinguish water from printed metal.

**Materials:** Satin black coating, fine surface variation, clear raised droplets of several sizes.

**Typography:** The physical label is the only type.

**Sound:** A filtered hiss opens slightly with the downward camera movement.

**Scroll interaction:** Water movement is authored on the timeline, so it retraces when scrolling backward.

**Transition:** A near droplet and dark structural edge briefly occlude the frame, leading into the same chamber at a larger scale.

## Shot 04 — The can becomes architecture

**Time / scroll:** 00:17–00:25 / 23–34%.

**Camera:** A widening tracking arc past close foreground ribs, with the lens still looking toward the product’s centre.

**Environment:** Concentric machined structures echo the rolled rim. A shallow liquid surface reflects their undersides. The space feels built, heavy and sealed.

**Product state:** A tall, mostly dark central mass; intermittent label fragments remain visible between ribs.

**Lighting:** White slits in the chamber and narrow reflections on the wet floor.

**Materials:** Rough black industrial surfaces, chrome edges, water-like reflective film.

**Typography:** None.

**Sound:** Low resonance acquires a subtle mechanical texture.

**Scroll interaction:** Parallax comes from actual camera translation past foreground geometry.

**Transition:** The camera moves between two ribs as the chamber begins closing around it.

## Shot 05 — Containment

**Time / scroll:** 00:25–00:33 / 34–45%.

**Camera:** The track tightens into a deliberate forward move toward a diagonal seam. Acceleration increases while the focal point remains stable.

**Environment:** Structural blades converge; the liquid reflection tightens into parallel bands. Motion has one direction and one cause.

**Product state:** The can is nearly concealed again. A reflected red slash is the clue connecting it to the enclosure.

**Lighting:** Red increasingly replaces white. Dark blades provide foreground occlusion rather than decorative particles.

**Materials:** Dense black metal, bright cut edges, tensioned reflective liquid.

**Typography:** None.

**Sound:** Pressure and filtered noise rise with progress; movement adds a brief, restrained rush.

**Scroll interaction:** The visitor physically advances the compression. No fake loading delay and no automatic scroll jump.

**Transition:** Two surfaces almost meet, reducing the image to a lightning-shaped seam.

## Shot 06 — BREACH

**Time / scroll:** 00:33–00:38 / 45–52%.

**Camera:** A fast, controlled retreat as the seam opens toward the lens, followed by a decelerating reframe.

**Environment:** The enclosure separates along the geometry of VOLD’s bolt. Large coherent pieces peel away; a restrained liquid ribbon follows the opening.

**Product state:** The silhouette is suddenly unobstructed, with the face still awaiting its key light.

**Lighting:** A brief red exposure peak, then immediate recovery into a clean white rim. No full-screen strobe.

**Materials:** Heavy architectural metal gives way to a thin, glossy liquid boundary.

**Typography:** None; the bolt is expressed through the space itself.

**Sound:** One compact low impact and a mechanical release. Reverse traversal uses a rising suction cue, with crossing hysteresis to avoid repeated hits while hovering at the threshold.

**Scroll interaction:** This event is continuous and reversible. Slowing the wheel lets the visitor inspect how the chamber opens.

**Transition:** The retreat becomes the product reveal’s wider camera arc.

## Shot 07 — The original

**Time / scroll:** 00:38–00:46 / 52–62%.

**Camera:** Pull out and arc into a low three-quarter pack shot. Controlled perspective gives the can mass without distorting it.

**Environment:** Open chamber, wet floor, separated structures held at the edges of the composition.

**Product state:** First complete Classic can. The label becomes readable; lid, rolled rims and condensation remain visible.

**Lighting:** Broad, soft white key; thin red edge; floor reflection beneath the product.

**Materials:** Satin printed metal contrasts with polished aluminium and cold droplets.

**Typography:** A small “CLASSIC / ENERGY DRINK” identifier, subordinate to the pack.

**Sound:** Pressure releases into a wider, calmer low-frequency space.

**Scroll interaction:** The visitor can hold the earned reveal or continue the same camera arc.

**Transition:** A remaining structural blade crosses the lens, providing a physical transition into green.

## Shot 08 — Green has teeth

**Time / scroll:** 00:46–00:52 / 62–71%.

**Camera:** Lateral tracking through sharper architectural fins, passing close to one foreground edge.

**Environment:** A vertical, faceted space replaces the circular enclosure. Green light travels along its edges in a controlled sequence.

**Product state:** Green Apple; the pack remains the central spatial anchor.

**Lighting:** Hard green grazing light and a clean white frontal reflection to preserve label readability.

**Materials:** Cut dark metal, polished planes and short, sharp reflection bands.

**Typography:** “GREEN APPLE / ENERGY DRINK”.

**Sound:** A dry electrical texture, with shorter attacks than Classic’s pressure bed.

**Scroll interaction:** Fins open in a directional sequence tied to travel, not random animation.

**Transition:** The camera passes the last fin as it turns from a sharp black surface into a warm metallic ribbon.

## Shot 09 — Heat, without fire

**Time / scroll:** 00:52–00:58 / 71–79%.

**Camera:** A rising arc following the ribbon’s curve, exposing more of the top of the can.

**Environment:** Warm, curved bands travel through a broader space with slower, weightier motion.

**Product state:** Ginger Ale, clearly identified as a mixer rather than an energy drink.

**Lighting:** Amber side light and warm white highlights; the centre remains dark and rich.

**Materials:** Copper-like reflections, glossy amber liquid film and satin packaging.

**Typography:** “GINGER ALE / SPARKLING MIXER”.

**Sound:** A warmer mechanical timbre, softened noise and a small liquid movement cue.

**Scroll interaction:** The ribbon unwraps as the camera rises; the same curve wraps again in reverse.

**Transition:** Its brightest reflection expands across the lens and resolves as the edge of a cool, transparent-looking panel.

## Shot 10 — Clear the air

**Time / scroll:** 00:58–01:04 / 79–87%.

**Camera:** A smooth downward track through widely spaced vertical planes. Less camera acceleration, more depth.

**Environment:** Cool, open space. Glass-like edges and sparse, purposeful bubbles replace metal compression.

**Product state:** Tonic Water, another distinct mixer.

**Lighting:** Cyan edge light, neutral key and clean highlights with more negative space.

**Materials:** Glass-like surfaces, clear liquid cues, polished aluminium; no frosted UI panels.

**Typography:** “TONIC WATER / SPARKLING MIXER”.

**Sound:** Airier filtered noise, then a steady reduction toward silence.

**Scroll interaction:** The planes separate and bubble paths progress with the camera; pausing arrests the sequence.

**Transition:** Foreground reflections leave the image until only the product’s rim remains.

## Shot 11 — The quiet

**Time / scroll:** 01:04–01:10 / 87–95%.

**Camera:** Settle from the moving arc into a nearly frontal view. A very short, slow retreat creates breathing room.

**Environment:** Architecture withdraws into black. The frame stops asking for attention.

**Product state:** Return to the original black Classic can, isolated and still legible.

**Lighting:** One white edge and a restrained frontal reflection.

**Materials:** The product remains physical; the environment is almost gone.

**Typography:** “NO RULES.” appears first. A real interval of scroll follows before “JUST VOLD.” appears.

**Sound:** The bed falls away. Silence gives the words their weight.

**Scroll interaction:** The visitor controls the pause between the two phrases.

**Transition:** The title settles into the final product composition as the lens eases farther back.

## Shot 12 — VOLD remains

**Time / scroll:** 01:10–01:14 / 95–100%.

**Camera:** A final small pull-away; no endlessly floating hero loop.

**Environment:** A dark, reflective stage and almost nothing else.

**Product state:** Final clean pack shot. VOLD remains as the chamber disappears.

**Lighting:** Balanced white product lighting with a final red accent.

**Materials:** Printed black metal, cold aluminium and a restrained floor reflection.

**Typography:** The finished brand statement, then “Explore the drinks”, “Stock VOLD” and replay.

**Sound:** Silence, apart from a brief deliberate replay cue if requested.

**Scroll interaction:** Further scrolling releases the film into useful brand and product content. Scrolling back re-enters the final composition at the same position.

**Transition:** The cinematic stage gives way to the existing product information, photography and enquiry flow.

## Production and interaction contract

Use the existing Vite, React, TypeScript, R3F, Drei, GSAP and Lenis stack. A framework migration would not improve the film. One camera follows an authored spline with a separate focal target; a single normalised progress value drives the lens, product and every major transformation. No screenshot can show the reversible breach.

The realtime build will use shaped product geometry, locally generated label and surface textures, detailed lid/rim/tab meshes, instanced condensation, precomputed reflection lighting, simple environment geometry and fragment-shader surface effects. It will not attempt a live full fluid simulation or path-traced rendering. The reference’s offline CGI quality is the visual target; equivalent fidelity cannot be promised from a browser running on integrated graphics.

Canvas DPR stays at or below 1.5 and antialiasing remains off. Mobile lowers reflection resolution and droplet counts while retaining the same narrative. Rendering pauses when hidden or offscreen. All major animation is progress-driven so reverse traversal stays coherent. Audio begins only after a visitor gesture and has an obvious mute control.

Reduced-motion mode offers held key compositions and direct shot navigation. The film always has a keyboard-operable scrubber and a way to skip to product information. These controls are quiet, readable and usable; they are not decorative dashboard chrome.

The existing Rulebreaker version is preserved at the requested GitHub repository. Its pull-and-rewind game remains a separate creative direction; it is not inserted into this film as another interaction.
